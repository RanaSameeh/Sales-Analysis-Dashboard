# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer distribution, and product performance through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

![Sales Dashboard](Sales%20Dashboard.png)

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, helping decision-makers monitor overall performance, identify trends, and evaluate profitability across different categories and regions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor total profit and sales quantity.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Identify Top 10 and Bottom 10 products.
- Analyze customer distribution by state.
- Evaluate discount impact on profitability.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Interactive slicers for Category, Year, and Quarter.
- KPI Cards:
  - Total Sales
  - Total Quantity
  - Total Profit
  - Average Discount
- Sales & Profit by Category
- Quantity by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Top 10 Products
- Bottom 10 Products
- Customer Distribution Map

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
